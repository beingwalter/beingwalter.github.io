Rest Assured

we start out sane,
but take on the crazy
in the midst of the stampeding herd.

we carry on,
and we’re carried along
as desire for more is stirred.

yet if one looks beyond
the rear to their front,
looking up reveals a bird.

abandon all seeking
to soar freely above,
fulfilled and at peace…

One Without a Second

labels smother,
as there is no other.

no father or mother,
sister or brother.
no friend or foe,
no one higher or low.

don’t bother to name,
as all are the same,
frame all as One,
see this and be done.

being done with separation,
with terms or relation,
reveals a quiet elation,
regardless of role,
each representing
a part of the whole...

a particular perspective,
be it joyful or invective,
no expression is defective,
just what is... here and now.

with no labels to smother,
there can be no other.

many pitch a tent at the gate,
content to wait in relative comfort.

Unpublished
Belling the Cat

lost in thought, I am caught
in this cat-and-mouse game,
when mind offers fear
I am once again tamed
and escape to the known,
to the comfort of names.

yet one voice is heard,
which is largely unheeded:
“BELL THE CAT,” it intones;
“BELL THE CAT,” is repeated,
as I turn away from the thought
feeling lost and defeated.

the voice comes again:
“BELL THE CAT,” it invites...
yet does it not know
the cat scratches and bites?
“BELL THE CAT,” once again…
dare I take up the fight?

and now comes the fear!
“BELL THE CAT,” issues forth
from my own very mind;
as I turn ’round and find
THE CAT looming large,
crouched and ready to charge.

“BELL THE CAT,” in my mind
as I step into the fear
and find peace at the center:
no 'me' or 'cat' here,
no bell hung to knell,
all is now clear.

the illusory cat,
when kept at a distance,
seemed solid and real
due to resistance.
step into the fear...
it has no existence!

Upcoming Anthology
Ending Pretending

The pretender finds it strange-
the very catalyst for change
is to inquire and clearly see
their unreality...
What is seen exposes ME,
then comes the urge to flee.
But to end pretending: BE.

Yet many will take flight
and seek refuge in twilight,
feeling something requires mending-
all the while pretending
to be a person with concerns.
Pivot, pivot, turn,
as yearning ever burns.

The pretender meets their end
when that one ceases to pretend.
And what is always shining forth
reveals eternal, endless mirth-
indeed, the death of dearth.
What’s birthed remains unborn,
as the perpetual dawning morn.
